## Summary of Results



1\. A total of 100 books were scraped from the first five pages from the website.

2\. Most books are priced between £20 and £50.

3\. Ratings of 3–5 are more common in the books than that of ratings 1 or 2.

4\. There is no as such strong relationship between book price and rating.

5\. Some categories have higher average prices than the other ones.

6\. Books that have high ratings as well as lower prices have the highest affordability scores.

7\. Most books are in stock, with varying stock quantities.
